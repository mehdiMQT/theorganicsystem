Questions founders ask me

My videos get views, but no leads.

This is the one I hear most. Views without leads means the video pulls the wrong viewer, or never tells the right one what to do next. Every Sprint video is picked from questions your buyers ask on calls, and ends with one clear step to your calendar.

I've tried YouTube. It doesn't bring clients in my business.

That's what Riley thought. Good videos, a few hundred views each. We didn't touch the video, only the title and thumbnail, and it's now past 26K views. He booked 8 calls. Most channels don't have a content problem. They have a packaging problem.

I don't know what videos to make, or who they're for.

Most founders I talk to are stuck between two or three audiences, so every video half-fits all of them. We pick one: the people who already pay you. Then every topic comes from what they ask before they buy.

I'm too busy to stay consistent.

Your part is 60 to 90 minutes a week: film one video from an outline I send 48 hours ahead. Ideas, titles, thumbnails and hooks are on me. And week 1 needs no filming at all, so your first results come before you've filmed anything.

My setup isn't good enough.

A phone and a window are enough. Buyers book because you understand their problem, not because of your lighting. The outline has your hook written, so you never sit in front of the camera wondering what to say.

Networking and referrals already work for me.

Keep them. They stop the moment you stop showing up. A video keeps working while you sleep: Houda's channel kept growing a year after we stopped working together.

I already post short-form. Isn't that enough?

Shorts get you seen. They rarely get you booked. Nobody pays thousands after 30 seconds, but they will after 15 minutes of watching you solve their exact problem. We keep your shorts and use them to send people to the long videos that sell.

I get clients from outreach and DMs.

Then you know the ceiling: message limits, ignored DMs, hours of your week. YouTube flips it. People reach out first, already knowing what you do. Keep outreach running while the channel builds.

I already run ads. Why YouTube?

Keep the ads. But they stop the day you stop paying. A good video keeps bringing people in for free, and they arrive already trusting you. That's the "organic" in The Organic System: clients who find you without you paying for every click.

My niche is too small for YouTube.

You don't need a big audience. You need the right few hundred people. Riley's 8 calls came from a channel that averaged a few hundred views a video. And one of my own niche videos barely got views, but one of the people who watched it was Iman Gadzhi's Head of Brand. That turned into a call with Iman himself.

What if it doesn't work?

Every video is measured against your own channel average at weeks 3 and 6, so you see exactly what's working and what we change. And if anything from my side is late, that week is free.
