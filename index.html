This isn't for everyone

It's for you if
  • You already sell a service, coaching or a program, and clients pay you.
  • You post on YouTube (or are ready to), but the videos don't bring calls.
  • You can film one video a week if someone hands you the plan.
It's not for you if
  • You don't have an offer yet.
  • You want to go viral overnight.
  • You won't get on camera.
